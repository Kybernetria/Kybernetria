About Kybernetria

Cybernetics, collective intelligence, and the systems we build together.

Kybernetria brings together writing and open-source tools about cybernetics, collective intelligence, and how people organise. It is a place to explore these ideas through both theory and practical work.

Systems in context

Complex systems are shaped by relationships, feedback, and context. Understanding them means looking beyond individual parts to how information moves, how decisions are made, and how a system changes over time.

The same questions apply to software, communities, and institutions: who can act, who can respond, and what happens when conditions change?

Collective intelligence

People coordinate through shared knowledge, local experience, and the ability to learn from one another. Tools can support that process by making information easier to share and decisions easier to understand.

This is the connection between cybernetics and collective organisation that runs through Kybernetria: how feedback and coordination can help people shape the systems they depend on.

Technology and agency

Technical choices have consequences for who holds power and who gets to participate. A system built around central control creates different possibilities from one that people can inspect, adapt, and govern together.

The interest here is in tools and approaches that give people more agency over their lives, alongside a critical look at technologies that limit it.

Writing and tools

The writing explores these questions through essays, examples, and historical projects. The projects put ideas into practice through open-source software.

Both are ways of working through the same questions: how systems behave, whose needs they serve, and how they might work better.
